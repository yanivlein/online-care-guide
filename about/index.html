About Online Care Guide

Online Care Guide is a starting point for researching online weight management and menopause care. We bring together focused guides and comparison resources to help you understand what different services offer and decide where to continue your research.

The topics we cover

Weight Management & GLP-1

This route takes you to ConsumerMed, a related comparison website covering online prescription weight management programs, including GLP-1 services. Explore information about program costs, consultation options, and ongoing support.

How to use the guide

Start with the topic you want to research. Consider practical details such as what a program includes, how consultations work, and what follow-up is described. When you find a provider you want to explore, visit its own website to check current prices, availability, eligibility requirements, and terms.

Our role

We provide general information and routes to comparison resources. We do not provide medical consultations, diagnoses, prescriptions, or personalized treatment recommendations. Questions about your health or the suitability of a treatment belong with a licensed healthcare professional.
