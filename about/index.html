About ConsumerMed

Finding online healthcare options can be confusing. Programs may look similar at first, but they can differ in eligibility requirements, treatment approaches, clinician access, pricing, and ongoing support. ConsumerMed.org brings these details together to make it easier to compare services and know what to ask before taking the next step.

We are an informational comparison website. We help visitors explore options offered by independent healthcare providers and telehealth services. We do not diagnose conditions, prescribe medications, provide treatment, or make personalized medical decisions.

What We Cover

Weight Management & GLP-1

Our weight management guides compare online services that may offer prescription weight-loss medications, including injectable GLP-1-based options, when clinically appropriate. We look at factors such as the medical evaluation process, medication access, ongoing support, pricing, and insurance-related information. Not every service offers the same medications, and eligibility is determined by the treating clinician.

Menopause, Perimenopause & HRT

Our women’s health guides explore online services for perimenopause, menopause, hormone replacement therapy (HRT), and related concerns such as changes in sexual desire. Depending on the service, options may include FDA-approved prescription treatments, nonhormonal approaches, and compounded medications. These options are not interchangeable, and a licensed clinician should help you understand their differences.

How to Use Our Comparisons

Start with the topic that matches your question. Compare providers based on the details that matter to you, such as consultation access, care format, total cost, medication or treatment options, and follow-up. Read our guides for context, then visit a provider’s official website to check current terms and availability.

Before beginning treatment, speak with a licensed healthcare professional about your symptoms, health history, eligibility, risks, and alternatives. A ranking or label on ConsumerMed is not a clinical recommendation for an individual reader.

How We Evaluate Services

We organize publicly available provider information and consider factors including clinical approach, clinician access, safety-related information, pricing clarity, ease of use, and the support a provider describes. Different criteria matter for weight management and for menopause or sexual health services. Our How We Rate page explains the approach and its limitations.
