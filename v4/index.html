What to check before you sign up

Online services work differently. These questions help you compare them on equal terms.

  1. The total cost

    Membership, medication, lab and shipping fees may be billed separately. Check what renews automatically and how to cancel.

  2. How you'll see a clinician

    Some services offer video or phone visits. Others rely on online questionnaires and messaging.

  3. What you'll be prescribed

    Ask whether a medication is brand-name, generic or compounded. Compounded medications are not FDA-approved.

  4. Who handles follow-up

    Find out who monitors your progress, and keep your regular doctor and pharmacy informed.
