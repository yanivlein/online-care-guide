1. Our Role and the Scope of This Policy

ConsumerMed publishes information, comparisons, and links to independent healthcare providers. It is not a medical practice, pharmacy, or patient portal. This policy concerns information handled through our Site and our own contact channels. When you follow a link to a healthcare provider or other third-party website, that destination’s privacy policy and terms govern its collection and use of information.

2. Information You Choose to Provide

If you email us, we receive your email address, the message, and any name, attachments, or other information you choose to include. We use this information to reply, investigate questions or corrections, administer business inquiries, and maintain appropriate records.

Please do not use our general contact email to send medical records, detailed symptoms, prescription information, payment card numbers, or other sensitive information that is unnecessary for your inquiry. If you voluntarily include such details, they become part of the correspondence we receive.

The Site has no contact forms, newsletter sign-ups, quizzes, or accounts. Email is the only way to send us information.

3. Technical and Usage Information

When you request a page, website hosting and security systems process technical information needed to deliver it. Depending on the configuration of the Site, such information may include your IP address, browser and device information, requested page, referring page, time of access, and diagnostic or security events.

The Site is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. GitHub states that it logs and stores the IP address of visitors to GitHub Pages sites for security purposes. GitHub handles that information under its own General Privacy Statement (opens in a new tab). We do not add our own analytics, usage measurement, or visitor logging to the Site.

4. Cookies, Analytics, and Similar Technologies

The Site does not set cookies or use browser storage, analytics, advertising pixels, or referral tracking tools. Our links to ConsumerMed comparisons and other websites do not carry tracking parameters added by us.

When you follow a link to another website, your browser may tell that website which page you came from, and its own cookies and privacy practices apply from that point.

You can usually control or delete browser cookies through your browser settings. Where applicable law requires additional consent or a choice about tracking, we will provide the relevant controls.

5. Third-Party Fonts, Hosting, and Embedded Services

The Site uses Google Fonts to display its typefaces. When a page loads, your browser requests the font files from Google’s servers, and Google receives your IP address and standard request information needed to deliver them.

Google states that data collected through the Google Fonts Web API is not used to create end-user profiles or for advertising. For more information, read Google’s explanation of privacy and Google Fonts (opens in a new tab) and Google’s Privacy Policy (opens in a new tab).

Apart from Google Fonts, the Site’s pages, images, and styles are served from the Site itself. It does not embed videos, forms, or other third-party services.

6. How We Use Information

We process information as reasonably needed to provide the Site, respond to messages, maintain security, address technical issues, and comply with applicable legal obligations. We do not use general website correspondence to provide medical treatment or determine whether a prescription is appropriate.

7. When Information May Be Disclosed

We may share relevant information with vendors that help provide hosting, email, security, or other approved site operations; with parties involved in administering a verified referral or advertising arrangement; and when disclosure is required by law or reasonably necessary to protect the Site or respond to misuse. A business transfer may involve information subject to applicable protections and law.

The providers linked from our pages are independent. Information that you submit directly to them is collected and handled under their own policies, not ours. The Site itself has no forms and does not send visitor information or lead details to the providers or partners we link to.
