Privacy Requests

You may use the same address, info@makegems.com, for questions about how we handle information or to make a privacy request. Write “Privacy request” in the subject line and describe what you are asking about. We may need limited additional information to verify a request when legally necessary. Please do not send identity documents unless we specifically provide a secure, appropriate method.

Medical and Provider Support

ConsumerMed is not a healthcare provider. We cannot review medical records, determine treatment eligibility, change prescriptions, access provider accounts, cancel subscriptions, or issue refunds on behalf of a provider.

For a treatment, prescription, billing, shipping, or account question, contact the healthcare company or clinician responsible for your care. If you believe you are experiencing a medical emergency in the United States, call 911 or seek immediate emergency care.

Please Protect Your Privacy

Do not email us medical records, prescription images, passwords, payment card numbers, or detailed health histories. Send only the information needed for the website-related question.
